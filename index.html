FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions?

Answers to common questions about starting therapy, what to expect, and how Mentara supports your mental wellness journey.

Mental health covers our emotional, psychological and social wellbeing. It shapes how we think, feel and handle everyday life, so caring for it matters just as much as physical health.

Small, consistent habits help most: regular sleep, movement, honest conversation, and professional support when things feel heavy. Therapy can help you build a plan that fits your life.

Persistent sadness, withdrawing from people you love, trouble sleeping or concentrating, and constant worry are common signs it may help to talk to someone.

Start with what you need: a specific specialty, a communication style, or scheduling flexibility. Most people meet a couple of therapists before finding the right fit, and that's okay.

Individual and group therapy, family counseling, workshops and self-guided tools all fall under mental wellness resources. Mentara combines several depending on your goals.

Listen without judgment, check in consistently, and gently encourage professional support if things don't improve. Just being present makes a real difference.
